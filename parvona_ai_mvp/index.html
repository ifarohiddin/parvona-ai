THE PROBLEM

One caregiver cannot watch every camera at every second.

Existing CCTV systems record events, but they do not actively help staff understand what may require attention. Parvona AI is designed as an intelligent monitoring layer.

01

Observe

Analyze uploaded or live video feeds and detect people, movement and scene activity.

02

Understand

Evaluate event patterns and flag situations that may require a human review.

03

Respond

Surface alerts in a dashboard so authorized staff can react more quickly.
